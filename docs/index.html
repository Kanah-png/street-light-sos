Multi Scenario Emergency Perception

Dual YOLO object and pose estimation models trained for six critical urban incidents.

🚗

Road Accident

Detects sudden velocity drops and vehicle bounding box overlaps with collision scoring.

🧘‍♂️

Unconscious Person

Tracks body torso angles relative to the ground using keypoint estimation to find fallen pedestrians.

🥊

Physical Assault

Monitors spatial proximity and rapid kinetic motion energy fluctuations indicating physical violence.

🔥

Fire and Smoke

Uses specialized vision classifiers to detect fire and expanding smoke in public street views.

🚨

Forced Abduction

Identifies rapid forced movement vectors between human subjects and nearby suspicious vehicles.

🚗

Traffic Stagnation

Monitors long term vehicle accumulation to alert central control of severe gridlocks.
